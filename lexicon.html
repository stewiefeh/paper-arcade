How Lexicon works

  1. Type any five-letter word and press Enter.
  2. Every letter gets stamped. Green means it is in the right slot, orange means the word holds that letter elsewhere, and plain paper means it is not in the word at all.
  3. You get six attempts. The daily word is the same for everyone on the same calendar day and rerolls at midnight.
  4. Free play rerolls instantly, as many times as you like, and does not touch your daily streak.

The dictionary is a hand-picked list of common five-letter words. Plurals and obscurities are mostly left out, which is a feature until you type one.
