House rules

Short, boring, and worth reading once.

  1. Nothing is collected

    Every score, streak, and best time is written to your browser's local storage. There is no server holding a copy, and clearing your browser data clears the record.

  2. Nothing is sold

    No ads, no popups, no "premium" tier. The games end when you stop playing them, which is the correct behavior for games and, frankly, for most things.

  3. Everything is readable

    Full keyboard support on every game, visible focus states, screen reader labels, and a reduced-motion mode that stills the whole press room when your OS asks for it.

  4. Everything is inspectable

    Hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no frameworks, no build step. The source is public, the comments are real, and the colophon lists the whole toolkit.

Paper Arcade is one portfolio project among several, but the only one that answers back when you poke it. The motion is a single kinetic headline and a lot of small, honest presses.
